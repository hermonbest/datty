## Deck: Photo Prompts (Capture Your Memories)
> Subtitle: "Capture your memories."

1. Show me the very oldest photo of yourself saved on your current phone, and tell me the story behind it.
2. Find and show me a candid photo of us where you think I look effortlessly attractive.
3. Share a picture from your camera roll that instantly makes you laugh out loud no matter how many times you see it.
4. Show me a photo of a place you’ve traveled to that took your breath away in person.
5. Take a photo right now of something in your immediate surroundings that reminds you of my love.
6. Find a screenshot of a funny, sweet, or chaotic text conversation you saved because you never want to forget it.
7. Show me a picture of your favorite meal you’ve ever eaten or cooked in your entire life.
8. Share a photo from a day when you felt completely in your element and proud of who you are.
9. Find a photo of a pet, animal, or nature moment that gave you pure, unadulterated joy.
10. Show me the most aesthetically pleasing or artistic photo you’ve ever taken on your phone.
11. Share a photo representing a dream or goal on your vision board for our future.
12. Find a photo of us that you haven't posted anywhere, but is secretly your favorite picture of our journey.
13. Take a snapshot of your current outfit or shoe check, including your favorite accessory.
14. Show me a photo from a time when things went completely wrong, but turned into an unforgettable memory.
15. Share a picture of a book, album, or piece of art that profoundly altered your perspective.
16. Find a childhood photo where your personality was on full, unapologetic display.
17. Show me the most recent photo you took before we started answering these questions.
18. Take a close-up photo of your favorite feature on my face right now.
19. Share a picture of a sunrise, sunset, or night sky that you wish we had watched together in person.
20. Find a photo from a family gathering or celebration that captures the heart of your loved ones.
21. Show me a meme saved in your gallery that perfectly describes our dynamic.
22. Take a photo of the book you're currently reading, your current workstation, or your current beverage.
23. Share a photo that represents a major turning point or milestone in your career or life.
24. Find a photo of us where we were both completely exhausted but having the time of our lives.
25. Take a spontaneous Polaroid-style photo right now capturing this exact moment between us.
26. Take a photo of something that currently represents “us” in an ordinary, non-performative way.
27. Capture the detail of my face or hands that you find yourself looking at most often.
28. Photograph the exact spot or object that holds a private memory only we understand.
29. Take a picture of the version of home that exists when it’s just the two of us.
30. Capture something that feels like a quiet promise or future you’re looking forward to.
31. Photograph the evidence of a shared ritual or habit that makes our days feel like ours.
32. Take a photo that shows the texture of an ordinary good moment we’d otherwise forget.
33. Capture something that represents how safe or free you feel with me.
34. Photograph the light, object, or corner that feels most like “our” atmosphere.
35. Take a picture of whatever currently makes you feel most grateful for this chapter.

## Deck: Photo Prompts (Flick Them Up)
> Subtitle: "Flick them up."

1. Take a selfie making the most dramatically exaggerated shocked expression you can muster in 3 seconds.
2. Snap a photo of the most chaotic or messy corner of your room with zero staging or cleaning.
3. Take a mirror selfie showing off your favorite physical feature today.
4. Capture a photo from an ultra-low, cinematic angle that makes a boring everyday object look epic.
5. Take a candid photo of me when I’m not looking and show me what you captured.
6. Snap a photo of your shoes and my shoes next to each other right now.
7. Take a dynamic 0.5x ultra-wide lens selfie of both of us making goofy faces.
8. Capture a photo of the sky directly above where you are standing right now.
9. Take a photo holding up the object within arm's reach that you could not live without today.
10. Snap a high-fashion editorial pose using whatever prop is closest to you.
11. Take a photo of your current view from your window or doorway.
12. Capture a close-up texture shot (a blanket, skin, foliage, wood grain) with rich lighting.
13. Take a selfie with your absolute best 'smize' (smiling with your eyes) without showing teeth.
14. Snap a picture of the tastiest thing currently in your fridge or pantry.
15. Take a photo of both of our hands clasped together with natural window lighting.
16. Capture a shadow silhouette photo of yourself on the wall or floor.
17. Take a 3-second rapid selfie timer photo doing a spontaneous synchronized couple pose.
18. Snap a photo of something bright red, bright blue, or bright yellow nearby.
19. Take a picture of your current cup, mug, or water bottle as if you're reviewing it for a magazine.
20. Capture a candid snapshot of your genuine smile when I tell you a compliment right now.
21. Take a photo looking down at your feet standing on an interesting floor pattern or natural ground.
22. Snap a selfie where only half of your face is in the frame, bathed in moody lighting.
23. Take a funny photo pretending to be a security camera catching you doing something mischievous.
24. Capture a snapshot of the most comforting nook in your living space.
25. Take our signature couple selfie of the day and set it as your lock screen for the next 24 hours.
26. Send me a photo of something that turned you on today (object, moment, memory, or body).
27. Capture the look or expression you wish I could see on your face right now.
28. Photograph something that makes you think of the last time we were really charged together.
29. Take a picture that represents the fantasy or tension you’re currently carrying.
30. Capture a detail of your body or space that feels private and meant for me.
31. Send a photo that says “I want you” without any words needed.
32. Photograph the aftermath or evidence of desire—rumpled sheets, a mark, a chosen outfit.
33. Capture something that feels forbidden, teasing, or just out of reach right now.
34. Take a picture of the version of yourself you only fully show me when things heat up.
35. Send a photo that is pure invitation—no explanation required.
