# Category: Future Plans & Dreams

## Deck: Future Plans & Dreams (Dream Big Together)
> Subtitle: "Dream big together."

1. If money, visas, and obligations were zero object, what does our dream home and location look like?
2. What is a massive, audacious bucket-list expedition you want us to take across the world?
3. What kind of financial freedom and abundance do you envision us building together by age 40?
4. If we started an empire, business, or creative venture as a power couple, what would it be?
5. What is a luxury experience (a private island villa, Michelin-star tour, yacht charter) you want to share with me?
6. How do you envision our ideal daily lifestyle when we reach complete financial independence?
7. What kind of architectural style, interior design, and garden aesthetic matches our soul home?
8. What is a philanthropic project, foundation, or community impact you want us to fund and lead together?
9. If we could spend a sabbatical year living in three different countries, which countries are we picking?
10. What is a moonshot career achievement that you want me to cheer you on for as you reach the top?
11. How do you envision our legacy and influence on our extended family, friends, and community?
12. What kind of art, vehicles, library, or unique collection do you want to fill our dream estate with?
13. If we could own a second vacation home anywhere on the planet (mountains, coast, countryside), where is it?
14. What is a personal dream of yours that sounds completely crazy to others, but you know I will support?
15. How do you envision our relationship dynamic, physical health, and energy when we are in our 60s and 70s?
16. What is an extraordinary celebration or milestone party you want us to throw for our future anniversary?
17. What kind of mentors, visionary friends, and inspiring social circle do you want us to surround ourselves with?
18. If we wrote a best-selling book together about life, love, or our craft, what would the title be?
19. What is an adventure sport or thrill (skydiving, deep-sea diving, pilot license) you want to conquer together?
20. How will we ensure that our shared life remains an ongoing, thrilling adventure rather than settling into routine?
21. What is a dream investment or asset you want us to acquire that creates multi-generational freedom?
22. How do you picture the celebrations of our 25th and 50th anniversaries with the people we love most?
23. If a genie granted us the ability to build any custom paradise in the world, what are the top 3 features?
24. What is the greatest joint triumph you can clearly visualize us celebrating with a bottle of champagne?
25. What is one bold step we can take this month to put our biggest shared dream into motion?
26. If money, logistics, and fear were removed, what life would we be building five years from now?
27. What’s a shared adventure or chapter that still feels slightly out of reach but deeply wanted?
28. How do you imagine our days feeling when we’ve created more of the life we actually want?
29. What does “enough” success, freedom, or security look like so we can actually enjoy each other?
30. Which dream of yours do you most want me to believe in and help protect?
31. What kind of older couple do you hope we become—energy, dynamic, way of moving through the world?
32. Where do you still hold back from dreaming bigger because part of you doesn’t want to risk disappointment?
33. What’s one bold, slightly unreasonable future vision that still makes your chest feel open?
34. How do you want us to keep choosing each other as the dream evolves and life gets more complex?
35. If we wrote the next decade as a story, what would the emotional tone and major plot points be?

## Deck: Future Plans & Dreams (Write the Next Chapter)
> Subtitle: "Write the next chapter."

1. What are the top three concrete milestones we need to achieve together in the next 12 to 24 months?
2. What is our immediate financial roadmap: savings targets, investments, and debt elimination strategies?
3. Where do we want to be living one year from today, and what steps do we need to take to make that transition?
4. What is the next big commitment or relationship step we are preparing to take together?
5. How will we budget and plan for our next major travel trip or holiday adventure?
6. What is a tangible skill or certification each of us needs to acquire this year to level up our careers?
7. How will we structure our weekly schedule over the next 6 months to balance ambition and quality time?
8. What is a major purchase (car, equipment, property deposit) we are actively saving for right now?
9. How will we handle our next career pivot or job transition as a cohesive, supportive team?
10. What is a joint wellness or fitness goal with a specific deadline that we can start tracking this quarter?
11. How do we want to organize our shared finances, bank accounts, and joint budgeting systems?
12. What is a domestic upgrade or home improvement project we want to finish in the next 90 days?
13. What family or social commitments do we need to prepare for and align on in the upcoming months?
14. How will we evaluate our relationship progress and celebrate our quarterly wins as a couple?
15. What is one potential roadblock or obstacle in our near future, and what is our contingency game plan?
16. What is a habit or routine that didn't serve us last year that we are officially retiring in this new chapter?
17. How can we create a monthly 'State of the Union' check-in to review our goals, budget, and connection?
18. What is a personal project of yours that you need dedicated time and space from me to finish this year?
19. What is a shared creative or entrepreneurial project we can realistically launch within 6 months?
20. How do we plan to handle holiday seasons, travel splits, and family visits over the coming year?
21. What is an emergency fund goal we must maintain to feel completely insulated from economic stress?
22. What is the next book or course we should read and study together to strengthen our partnership?
23. What is a timeline for when we want to make major decisions about housing, marriage, or family expansion?
24. What is the single most important metric of success for our relationship over the next 365 days?
25. Let's write the first sentence of our next chapter right now: what is our core theme for the year ahead?
26. What does the next chapter of “us” need more of, and what does it need less of?
27. Which current habits or patterns will quietly sabotage the future we say we want if we don’t change them?
28. How do you want to feel in our relationship one year from now that you don’t fully feel yet?
29. What’s one concrete decision or experiment we could make in the next three months that would move us toward the life we want?
30. Where do you need more shared vision, and where do you need more individual freedom within the next chapter?
31. What are you most excited to build or experience with me that we haven’t fully started yet?
32. How do we protect the quality of our connection while we chase bigger external goals?
33. What does a thriving next chapter look like on an ordinary Tuesday, not just the highlight reel?
34. Which fears about the future are you ready to stop letting drive the car?
35. If we treated this next season as a deliberate co-authored story, what would we want the reader to feel by the end of it?
