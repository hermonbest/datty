# Category: Would You Rather?

## Deck: Would You Rather? (Choose Wisely or Don't)
> Subtitle: "Choose wisely (or don't)."

1. Would you rather always have to say everything that’s on your mind or never be able to speak again?
2. Would you rather always be 20 minutes early or 15 minutes late to every single life event?
3. Would you rather lose the ability to read or lose the ability to speak your native language?
4. Would you rather have a personal chef who cooks all meals or a personal driver who takes you anywhere?
5. Would you rather live without music for a year or without movies/TV shows for a year?
6. Would you rather accidentally reply-all to a company-wide email or text your boss a romantic message meant for me?
7. Would you rather explore the depths of the ocean or the far reaches of outer space together?
8. Would you rather always have bad Wi-Fi wherever you go or never have access to air conditioning/heating?
9. Would you rather have unlimited free flights anywhere in the world or unlimited free five-star dining?
10. Would you rather know the exact date of your death or know the exact cause of your death?
11. Would you rather spend a week in total isolation in nature or a week partying non-stop in a crowded city?
12. Would you rather have your browser history made public to your family or your gallery camera roll?
13. Would you rather wake up at 5:00 AM every day refreshed or sleep in until noon every day feeling tired?
14. Would you rather never have to do laundry again or never have to do dishes again?
15. Would you rather be able to pause time for 10 minutes once a day or rewind time by 1 minute once a day?
16. Would you rather be stuck in traffic with no radio for 4 hours or wait in a doctor's waiting room with no phone for 4 hours?
17. Would you rather live in a world where everyone knows your salary or where everyone knows your relationship secrets?
18. Would you rather always have to whisper or always have to shout when talking to me?
19. Would you rather lose all your photos from the past 5 years or lose all your saved bookmarks/notes?
20. Would you rather go on a luxurious vacation with an annoying group or camp in the rain just the two of us?
21. Would you rather have your ideal dream body without working out or have your ideal dream career without working long hours?
22. Would you rather fight your biggest fear once or live with mild anxiety every single day?
23. Would you rather never eat your favorite meal again or never hear your favorite song again?
24. Would you rather be able to teleport anywhere instantly or be able to fly at 60 mph?
25. Would you rather choose the exact house we live in or choose the exact city we live in forever?
26. Would you rather always know exactly what I’m thinking, or always be able to make me feel exactly what you want?
27. Would you rather have one perfect, unforgettable year with me or twenty good-but-ordinary ones?
28. Would you rather I challenge you every day or protect your peace at all costs?
29. Would you rather be completely understood or completely desired—if you could only fully have one?
30. Would you rather relive our best day on loop or keep creating new ones with no guarantee they’ll top it?
31. Would you rather I never leave your side or always give you the exact amount of space you need?
32. Would you rather know the exact day we’ll stop being “us,” or live in permanent uncertainty?
33. Would you rather I love the realest version of you or the version of you that makes life easiest?
34. Would you rather fight passionately and make up intensely, or never fight and never quite feel the high?
35. Would you rather be my safe place or my most exciting risk?

## Deck: Would You Rather? (Hypotheticals and Chaos)
> Subtitle: "Hypotheticals and chaos."

1. Would you rather fight off a zombie horde with a frying pan or survive in Jurassic Park with a laser pointer?
2. Would you rather have a narrator follow you around speaking your thoughts or background music play your mood 24/7?
3. Would you rather wake up speaking fluent Ancient Greek with no memory of English or wake up on Mars in a rover?
4. Would you rather be hunted by a snail that kills you if it touches you (it always knows your location) for $10M or live normally?
5. Would you rather have all animals understand your speech or have you understand all bird language?
6. Would you rather live in a simulated reality where you are a monarch or real life as an ordinary middle-class citizen?
7. Would you rather sneeze confetti every time you are turned on or toot glitter every time you get nervous?
8. Would you rather fight 5 average humans in an arena or 1 Silverback Gorilla that knows karate?
9. Would you rather be trapped in an elevator with your worst ex or your partner's most judgmental family member?
10. Would you rather have your life reset to age 10 with all your current knowledge or jump to age 50 with $50 million?
11. Would you rather swap bodies with me for 48 hours with no prep or swap bodies with your pet for 24 hours?
12. Would you rather always know when someone is lying to you or always get away with every lie you tell?
13. Would you rather survive on a deserted island with your favorite snacks or in a luxury penthouse with zero internet?
14. Would you rather have your hands permanently replaced with magnetic clamps or wheels for feet?
15. Would you rather every movie you ever watch turn into a musical or every book you read narrate itself in Gilbert Gottfried's voice?
16. Would you rather discover that magic is real but you can't use it, or discover aliens exist and they ignore Earth?
17. Would you rather have a tiny demon on your shoulder giving bad advice or a tiny angel who never stops judging you?
18. Would you rather live through a groundhog-day loop of our worst first date or a loop of our hardest exam day?
19. Would you rather be able to turn invisible only when screaming at the top of your lungs or fly only 2 inches off the ground?
20. Would you rather have your teeth glow in the dark neon green or your hair grow 2 inches every single day?
21. Would you rather fight a clone of yourself that has double your stamina or 3 clones with half your strength?
22. Would you rather never be able to wear shoes again or never be able to wear pants again in public?
23. Would you rather have the power to summon infinite pizza but it's always lukewarm, or summon infinite coffee that is scalding?
24. Would you rather spend 1 year in a submarine deep underwater or 1 year in an orbital space station alone?
25. Would you rather make any lie you tell become the reality for 1 hour or erase one historic event from history?
26. Would you rather we get caught in a completely compromising situation or never risk being seen that way at all?
27. Would you rather I tell you a hard truth that temporarily breaks us or keep a softer lie that lets us stay comfortable?
28. Would you rather live in a world where everyone knows our private dynamic or a world where no one ever sees how we really are?
29. Would you rather have the ability to pause time during our best moments or rewind and redo our worst ones?
30. Would you rather I become suddenly famous and our private life gets complicated, or we stay completely anonymous forever?
31. Would you rather lose all physical desire for each other but keep the emotional depth, or keep the heat but lose the deep knowing?
32. Would you rather I push every one of your buttons on purpose for a week, or treat you with perfect carefulness the whole time?
33. Would you rather we get one impossible wish granted as a couple, or keep the messy, real version of what we have?
34. Would you rather know every future version of us or stay surprised by who we become?
35. Would you rather I love you in the exact way you need, or in the exact way I naturally do?
