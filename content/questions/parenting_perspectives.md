# Category: Parenting Perspectives

## Deck: Parenting Perspectives
> Subtitle: "Future chaos coordinators."

1. What is your philosophy on handling toddler tantrums in public: calm ignoring, firm redirection, or immediate exit?
2. How do you envision dividing daily parenting duties and sleepless nights so neither partner burns out?
3. What is a boundary around technology, screen time, and social media you want to enforce for our future kids?
4. What kind of bedtime routine do you want to create to make evenings peaceful rather than chaotic?
5. How will we maintain our romantic connection and date nights when kids enter the equation?
6. What is your approach to discipline: logical consequences, gentle parenting, firm boundaries, or time-outs?
7. How do you plan to handle the chaotic mess, endless laundry, and clutter that comes with children?
8. What is your stance on extracurricular activities: letting kids try everything or focusing on 1 or 2 disciplines?
9. How will we handle disagreements about parenting choices when our kids are actively watching and listening?
10. What is a funny, chaotic memory from your own childhood that made your parents lose their minds?
11. How do you want to celebrate milestones, birthdays, and back-to-school moments to make them magical?
12. What is your strategy for teaching kids emotional regulation and how to name their big feelings?
13. How will we navigate food battles, picky eaters, and nutrition without turning dinner into a war zone?
14. What is your view on allowance, chores, and teaching kids the value of hard work from an early age?
15. How do we handle advice or boundary intrusions from in-laws and grandparents regarding our children?
16. What kind of family pets or outdoor adventures do you want to bring into our kids' daily lives?
17. How do we teach our children resilience and how to lose gracefully in games and competitions?
18. What is your approach to teaching kids body autonomy, consent, and personal privacy?
19. If our future teenager comes home past curfew smelling of something they shouldn't, what is our game plan?
20. How do we make sure our home is a chaotic hub of laughter, friends, and creativity where neighborhood kids love to hang out?
21. What is a parenting rule you swear you will never enforce, but secretly know you probably will?
22. How do we balance structured schedules with unstructured, boredom-fueled imaginative playtime?
23. What is one chaotic crisis scenario (vomit on a road trip, lost in an airport) and who takes the lead?
24. How will we teach our children how to apologize and take responsibility for hurting others?
25. What is the biggest source of joy you anticipate when you imagine our future home filled with little voices?
26. What kind of emotional climate do you most want any future kids to grow up inside?
27. Which of your own childhood wounds are you most determined not to pass down?
28. How do you imagine we’ll handle it when we inevitably disagree about parenting in the moment?
29. What does “good enough” parenting look like to you, free from perfectionism?
30. How do you want us to protect our relationship so it doesn’t get completely absorbed by kid logistics?
31. What values do you hope any children of ours absorb just by watching how we treat each other?
32. Where do you suspect you’ll need the most support or accountability as a parent?
33. How do you want to talk about the hard, messy, non-Instagram parts of raising humans?
34. What legacy of emotional safety or courage do you hope we create for the next generation?
35. If we parent the way we currently love each other, what would that give our kids—and what might it still lack?

## Deck: Parenting Perspectives
> Subtitle: "Legacy begins at home."

1. What are the top three core moral values that are completely non-negotiable in raising our family?
2. What traditions, holiday rituals, or cultural celebrations do you most want to pass down to the next generation?
3. How do you want our children to describe the emotional atmosphere of our home when they grow up?
4. What is an essential life skill (financial literacy, emotional intelligence, cooking, critical thinking) we must teach them?
5. How will we teach our children about diversity, empathy, and standing up for people who are marginalized?
6. What is a generational curse or unhealthy expectation from our lineages that will never enter our household?
7. How do you want to model a healthy, loving, communicative marriage in front of our children every day?
8. What role will spirituality, faith, or moral philosophy play in our family's foundation?
9. How will we nurture our children's unique individuality even if their passions differ completely from ours?
10. What kind of relationship do you hope to have with our children once they are grown adults with their own lives?
11. How will we teach our kids to have a healthy relationship with money, abundance, and generous giving?
12. What is a story from your family history or ancestral journey that you want our children to know by heart?
13. How do we create an environment where our kids feel safe coming to us with any mistake, without fear of harsh judgment?
14. What is your vision for family dinners, discussions around the table, and intellectual debate in our home?
15. How will we teach our children to handle peer pressure, societal standards, and staying true to their values?
16. What books, art, and music do you want to surround our children with to spark their lifelong wonder?
17. How will we handle passing down cultural heritage, native languages, and ethnic roots to our kids?
18. What is a quality in me that you cannot wait to see reflected in our future children?
19. How will we navigate teaching our children healthy skepticism, digital literacy, and independent thought?
20. What does 'building generational wealth' mean to you—financial assets, strong education, or emotional health?
21. How do we ensure each child feels uniquely celebrated and never compared to their siblings?
22. What legacy do you want our family name to carry in our wider community and society?
23. If you could write a one-sentence family mission statement for our future household, what would it be?
24. How will we prepare our children to leave home confident, capable, and ready to contribute to the world?
25. When our grandchildren look back at our life together, what do you want them to remember most?
26. What do you most want any future family of ours to feel in their bones about love and safety?
27. Which parts of your upbringing do you want to deliberately redeem or rewrite through how we show up?
28. How do you want the story of “us” to be told by the people who grow up watching it?
29. What everyday behaviors between us would you hope children absorb without us ever having to lecture?
30. Where do you still carry unfinished business with your own parents that could leak into how you parent?
31. What does a strong, healthy family culture look like to you beyond the usual clichés?
32. How do you want us to handle our own conflicts so that kids learn repair instead of rupture or silence?
33. What kind of emotional vocabulary and permission do you want the next generation to have that you didn’t?
34. If legacy is built in the small, repeated moments, which small moments matter most to you?
35. What do you hope people say about the home and partnership we built long after we’re gone?
