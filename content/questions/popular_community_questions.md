# Category: Popular Community Questions

## Deck: Daily Delight
> Subtitle: "Gratitude and vulnerability."

1. What was the single smallest thing that brought a smile to your face today?
2. When was the last time you felt truly understood without saying a word?
3. What is one quiet habit or gesture of mine that never fails to comfort you?
4. What is a recent compliment you received that you secretly replayed in your mind?
5. What part of your daily routine brings you unexpected peace?
6. What is something you feel deeply grateful for today that you took for granted five years ago?
7. What is one vulnerability you used to hide that you now consider a strength?
8. When do you feel most safe being completely yourself around me?
9. What song instantly lifts your mood no matter what kind of day you're having?
10. What is a kind thing a stranger did for you recently that stuck with you?
11. What is one emotion you find difficult to express, and how can I make it easier for you?
12. What simple sensory experience (a scent, sound, warmth) made you feel alive this week?
13. What is something you love about how our connection has evolved recently?
14. When did you last laugh until your stomach hurt, and what caused it?
15. What is a burden you’re carrying right now that you'd like to put down for an hour?
16. What made you feel genuinely proud of yourself in the past forty-eight hours?
17. What is your favorite memory of us from this past month?
18. If you could bottle up one feeling from this week to keep forever, which one would it be?
19. What is something you appreciate about your body today?
20. How do you prefer to be comforted when life feels overwhelming?
21. What is one dream or hope you've been hesitant to share out loud?
22. What is your favorite part of waking up next to / talking to each other?
23. What lesson from a difficult day recently turned into a moment of gratitude?
24. What is something small we did together recently that felt deeply romantic to you?
25. If you had to describe our connection today in three words, what would they be?
26. What small thing did I do recently that made you feel quietly cherished, even if you never said it out loud?
27. When was the last time you felt truly safe being soft with me, and what made that moment possible?
28. What’s one ordinary detail about our life together that still surprises you with gratitude?
29. If you could bottle one feeling I give you and keep it forever, which feeling would it be and why?
30. What’s something you’ve been holding back from saying because it feels too tender or exposing?
31. When do you feel most seen by me, and what am I doing in those moments?
32. What’s a quiet strength of mine that you’ve never fully told me how much you appreciate?
33. What’s one way I’ve made your life lighter that you worry I don’t realize?
34. If vulnerability had a texture or temperature between us, what would today’s version feel like?
35. What’s the most honest “thank you” you’ve wanted to give me but haven’t found the words for yet?
