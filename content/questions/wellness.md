# Category: Wellness

## Deck: Wellness
> Subtitle: "Babe, drink more water."

1. How many glasses of water have you actually drank today, and should we go get a full bottle right now?
2. What is your current sleep routine, and what is one small change that would dramatically improve your rest?
3. What is a healthy, nourishing meal that makes your body feel energized and vibrant every time you eat it?
4. How do you feel about our current workout or physical movement routine, and how can we keep each other moving?
5. What is a physical symptom (tight shoulders, headaches, lower back ache) your body uses to scream 'I need a break'?
6. What is a posture check you need right now: unclench your jaw, drop your shoulders, and breathe deeply?
7. How can we make cooking healthy, whole meals at home feel like an enjoyable couple ritual rather than a chore?
8. What is your relationship with caffeine, energy drinks, or sugar, and do you want to recalibrate?
9. What is an outdoor physical activity (hiking, swimming, cycling, long walks) that you want us to do every weekend?
10. How do you feel about your morning routine—does it leave you grounded or rushing out the door stressed?
11. What is one preventative health habit (stretching, daily vitamins, doctor checkups) you’ve been procrastinating on?
12. How can I gently encourage your physical wellness goals without making you feel monitored or judged?
13. What is your favorite way to unwind physically before bed (hot shower, herbal tea, gentle stretching)?
14. How does spending time in direct sunlight and fresh air impact your mood and daily energy levels?
15. What is a toxic dietary or fitness myth you grew up believing that you have successfully let go of?
16. What physical fitness milestone or strength goal would you love to hit by the end of this year?
17. How many hours of screen time did your phone record yesterday, and what is a realistic limit we can set?
18. What is a simple self-care habit that instantly restores your physical vitality when you are drained?
19. How do you feel about meal prepping on Sundays to make weekdays effortless and healthy?
20. What is your body's ideal amount of sleep per night to function at peak cognitive performance?
21. When was the last time you went for a 30-minute walk with zero headphones or podcasts, just taking in the environment?
22. What is a healthy snack you genuinely enjoy that we should always keep stocked in the kitchen?
23. How do you handle rest days—do you feel guilty for resting, and how can we reframe recovery as productive?
24. What is a wellness challenge (like a 30-day hydration or daily 10k steps challenge) we can tackle as a team?
25. If your body could write you a short letter right now, what would it thank you for and what would it request?
26. How is your body actually doing right now, beyond the automatic “fine”?
27. What does real rest look like for you, and how often are you actually getting it?
28. Where are you still overriding your body’s signals because life (or us) feels more important?
29. What’s one small physical care habit that would make a noticeable difference if you were consistent?
30. How do you want me to support your physical well-being without nagging or policing?
31. When do you feel most at home in your own skin, and how can we create more of that?
32. What’s the connection you’ve noticed between how you treat your body and how you show up emotionally with me?
33. Where is stress currently living in your body, and what does it need?
34. How do you want us to protect sleep, movement, and basic nourishment when everything else gets demanding?
35. What’s one kind, non-performative thing you could do for your body this week that you’d actually enjoy?

## Deck: Wellness
> Subtitle: "Healthy minds, healthy bodies."

1. What is currently taking up the most mental bandwidth and cognitive load in your mind today?
2. How do you practice emotional hygiene—clearing out mental clutter and intrusive worries?
3. What is your go-to grounding technique when you feel a sudden surge of anxiety or panic?
4. How has therapy, journaling, or self-reflection changed the way you understand your inner landscape?
5. What is a negative self-talk phrase that frequently plays in your head, and what is the compassionate antidote?
6. How does physical tension in your body mirror the emotional stress you are carrying in your mind?
7. What is an activity where you lose all sense of time and enter a complete state of psychological flow?
8. When was the last time you gave yourself permission to do absolutely nothing without feeling guilty?
9. What boundary do you need to set with news, media consumption, and social feeds to protect your mental peace?
10. How do you navigate seasonal shifts, gloomy weather, or low-energy phases of the year?
11. What is a breathing exercise or mindfulness tool that actually works for your nervous system?
12. How can I create a safe, judgment-free decompression zone for you when you return home after a grueling day?
13. What is something you used to be deeply embarrassed about that you now treat with total self-compassion?
14. How do you process anger or frustration in a somatic, healthy way that doesn't bottle it up inside?
15. What is the difference for you between healthy solitude that restores you vs. isolating yourself unhealthily?
16. What is a mantra, quote, or affirmation that consistently anchors your mind during chaotic seasons?
17. How do you balance high ambition with deep psychological contentment in the present moment?
18. What is a creative outlet (writing, music, building, gardening) that keeps your soul feeling vibrant?
19. Who in your life can you be 100% emotionally messy with, and what does that safety mean to you?
20. How do you recognize when you are entering a depressive or low-dopamine valley, and what is your protocol?
21. What role does gratitude journaling or intentional appreciation play in rewiring your brain's negativity bias?
22. How can we cultivate more playfulness, silliness, and laughter in our home to release somatic stress?
23. What is a limiting mental belief you are ready to evict from your mind permanently?
24. What does true inner peace look, feel, and sound like in your day-to-day life?
25. How can we make our home a sanctuary of psychological calm and emotional safety for both of us?
26. What’s currently weighing on your mental or emotional bandwidth that you haven’t fully shared?
27. How do you know when your nervous system is dysregulated, and what helps bring you back?
28. Where are you still using distraction or numbing instead of actual processing or rest?
29. What does mental and emotional hygiene look like for you in a sustainable way?
30. How has our relationship supported (or sometimes strained) your psychological well-being?
31. What’s one boundary with technology, news, or outside input that would protect your inner world?
32. When do you feel most mentally clear and emotionally resourced, and what conditions create that?
33. How do you want us to talk about mental health without it becoming heavy or clinical?
34. What’s the difference between supporting each other and becoming each other’s only emotional regulator?
35. If we treated our minds with the same care we (ideally) give our bodies, what would change first?
