# Category: Creative / Visual

## Deck: Stickers
> Subtitle: "Your inner Picasso."

1. Draw our current relationship vibe using only three basic emojis or shapes.
2. Design a custom sticker that represents your internal reaction when I text you.
3. Sketch a quick doodle of your favorite memory of us without lifting your pen.
4. If our dynamic was an animated cartoon duo, who would we be and what do we look like?
5. Draw a mascot that represents our shared sense of humor.
6. Design a billboard slogan and illustration celebrating our love story.
7. Sketch your dream date night as a mini comic strip in 3 panels.
8. If you could tattoo one tiny symbol on my wrist to remind me of you, what would you draw?
9. Draw how you feel right now after a long day vs. how you feel when we finally hang out.
10. Pick 3 physical objects around the room and create a sticker badge blending them together.
11. Doodle the ultimate comfort meal you want us to eat together tonight.
12. Draw my facial expression when I am trying really hard not to laugh.
13. Create a quirky superhero logo that captures your partner's top superpower.
14. Draw our imaginary future pet and give it the most chaotic name possible.
15. Sketch the floor plan of our fantasy vacation treehouse or beach cabin.
16. Design a custom warning label sticker that should come attached to you.
17. Draw a doodle representing the song that best describes our relationship.
18. Sketch what your brain feels like during your most productive hour of the day.
19. Draw a funny caricature of me doing my favorite daily habit.
20. Design an official postage stamp commemorating our anniversary.
21. Doodle what our vibe will look like when we are 80 years old.
22. Create a sticker emblem for our private 'two-person club'.
23. Sketch your ultimate safe haven or happy place in 60 seconds.
24. Draw how our personalities fit together like puzzle pieces.
25. Create a signature signature-style doodle that we can leave for each other as secret notes.
26. If your current emotional state were a piece of abstract art, what colors, shapes, and textures would it have?
27. Draw (or describe) the version of me that lives in your mind when you’re feeling most loved.
28. What symbol or simple image best represents the part of you that still feels wild and untamed?
29. If our relationship had a visual motif that only the two of us understood, what would it look like?
30. Sketch the emotional weather inside you right now—storm, clear sky, fog, or something stranger.
31. What color is the feeling you get when I look at you a certain way, and where does that color live in your body?
32. If you could design a sticker that captures your softest, most private self, what would be on it?
33. What’s the visual metaphor you’d use for the way we fit together when everything else falls away?
34. Draw the version of “home” that exists only when we’re alone together.
35. If your inner child handed me a drawing of how they see our connection, what would be on the page?
